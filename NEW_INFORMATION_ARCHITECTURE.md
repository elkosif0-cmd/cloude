# NEW INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Canonical capability organization: [docs/rebuild/02-capability-map.md](docs/rebuild/02-capability-map.md).  
Runtime fusion implementation: `ui/fusion-shell.js`.

The global product architecture is one shell organized around: Leadership/Command, Commercial Operations, Finance & Compliance, Intelligence & Analysis, Production & Quality, Accounting & Assurance, and System. Standard/Advanced is not a separate application mode; advanced capabilities remain discoverable inside their business context and Command Palette.
