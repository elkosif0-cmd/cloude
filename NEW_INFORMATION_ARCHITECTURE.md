# New Information Architecture

The product uses one global shell. “Standard vs Advanced” is no longer a primary navigation model.

## 1. القيادة
- Unified Command Center
- Attention queue
- Global command/search

## 2. التشغيل التجاري
- Catalog & pricing
- Offers/orders
- Customers
- Inventory
- Suppliers & purchasing
- Events and daily operations

## 3. المالية والامتثال
- Cash
- Receivables
- B2B credit
- ZATCA invoicing
- Financial/executive reports

## 4. الذكاء والتحليل
- Jev contextual decisions
- Sensitivity
- Sales reconciliation
- Profitability/risk analytics

## 5. الإنتاج والجودة
- Blending/manufacturing
- Labels/barcodes/scanner
- Traceability
- Cold chain
- Culinary/production controls

## 6. المحاسبة والمراجعة
- Audit command workspace
- Documents/evidence
- Issues
- Council
- Financial statements/adjustments
- Report/issuance
- Analytics
- Standards research

Audit sub-navigation is contextual inside this domain; it must not render a second full application shell.

## 7. النظام
- Settings
- Data/backup
- Health
- Permissions/capabilities
- Integrations/governance
