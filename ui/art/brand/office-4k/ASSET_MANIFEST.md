## Art direction
- Establish the office with a natural panoramic window and greenery, bridging the cinematic Earth intro to actual workflows.
- Forest green + mint/light sage + limestone + restrained sunlight. No neon-green saturation or overpowering gold.
- Title: Montserrat at carefully controlled weight/letter spacing; Arabic: self-hosted IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400/600/700.
- Explain each department as a goal-first entry, not a repeated crowded menu.
- These images are for approval only; do not alter the protected SQC:AUTH script or isolated company storage until the visual direction is reviewed and implemented as accessible working UI.

## Preview / reproduction
- Open `KOSIF_EARTH_Office_4K_Concept.html` in a browser at 3840×2160.
- Ensure sibling original JPG and the project’s `ui/art/fonts` directory exist. Browser/CSS preserves sharp HTML text and icons.
- The HTML preview depicts suggested look-and-feel; actual UI must use working DOM controls and real company context.
